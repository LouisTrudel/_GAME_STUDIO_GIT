## Visual Description
The "Goofy Dog" is a low-poly stylized character designed for a mobile-first Three.js game. It features exaggerated proportions—a large head compared to its body, floppy triangular ears, and a permanently wagging tongue.

## Colors
- Primary Fur: #FFD700 (Vibrant Yellow)
- Secondary/Ears: #FF8C00 (Dark Orange)
- Tongue/Accent: #FF4081 (Bright Pink)
- Nose/Eyes: #212121 (Dark Charcoal)

## Dimensions/Specs
- Poly Count: < 500 triangles
- Scale: 1 unit height
- Texture: Flat-shaded, no complex textures to ensure fast load times.

## Animation Guidelines
- Idle: Slight bobbing motion and ears flapping.
- Movement: Waddle animation with exaggerated, "heavy" footfalls.
- Expression: Tongue hangs out at all times; eyes jitter slightly to convey excitement.

## Mood
- Playful, energetic, and slightly clumsy.
