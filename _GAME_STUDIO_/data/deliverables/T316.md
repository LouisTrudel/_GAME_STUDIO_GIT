# AI Agent Studios & Multi-Agent Orchestration: Competitive Landscape

## Summary

The 2026 market has fragmented into three distinct categories: **orchestration frameworks** (CrewAI, LangGraph, AutoGen), **autonomous coding agents** (Devin, Claude Code, Cursor), and **app generation platforms** (Lovable, Bolt, Replit Agent). BOSS occupies a unique position: a **project-definition-to-delivery studio** with persistent memory, skill libraries, and multi-agent coordination. No direct competitor combines all three.
